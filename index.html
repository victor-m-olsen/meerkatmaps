About Us

Meerkat Maps is a consultancy service started by geographers working in the humanitarian world. We drive impact by helping social impact businesses use spatial data effectively. Combined we have over 15 years of experience working with NGOs, the United Nations, and private sector partners on geospatial tools, analysis and visual products.

Our small team is quite international, currently based in Lilongwe, Malawi, we support customers globally.
